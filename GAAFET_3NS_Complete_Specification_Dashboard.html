6. TCAD Simulation Decks & Solver Commands

Structure Editor (SDE Scheme Commands)

  • (sdegeo:create-cuboid ...)
    Creates 3D solid spatial primitives for nanosheets, oxide sleeves, inner spacers, metal gate shell, BDI, and substrate.
  • (sdedr:define-constant-profile ...)
    Defines constant Phosphorus ($1\times10^{20}\text{ cm}^{-3}$) and Boron ($1\times10^{15}\text{ cm}^{-3}$) doping regions.
  • (sdedr:define-analytical-profile-placement ...)
    Implements smooth Gaussian error function (Erf) lateral junction transitions with $\sigma_x = 2.0\text{ nm}$.
  • (sdedr:define-refinement-size ...)
    Configures anisotropic 3D mesh resolution ($\Delta x=1\text{nm}, \Delta y=1\text{nm}, \Delta z=0.5\text{nm}$ inside nanosheets).

Device Simulator (SDevice Physics Keywords)

  • eQuantumPotential(Density)
    Solves 1D/3D Density-Gradient quantum equations to account for quantum confinement in $4\text{ nm}$ thin sheets.
  • Mobility(Enormal(Lombardi))
    Models transverse electric field surface roughness & acoustic phonon mobility degradation at high gate bias.
  • Mobility(HighFieldSaturation)
    Canali model accounting for electron velocity saturation at high electric fields ($V_{DS} = 0.70\text{ V}$).
  • Math { Method = Super / ParDiSo }
    64-bit parallel sparse direct solver utilizing 4 CPU threads with damped Newton-Raphson iterations.
7. Glossary of Terms & Semiconductor Acronyms
GAAFET Architecture
Gate-All-Around Field-Effect Transistor: A 3D transistor design where the gate dielectric and conductor wrap completely around all 4 sides of the channel bodies, providing maximum electrostatic control over short-channel effects.
BDI Oxide Isolation
Bottom Dielectric Isolation Oxide: An ultra-thin insulating dielectric plate ($\text{SiO}_2$) placed between the lowest nanosheet's gate metal and the bulk silicon substrate to eliminate sub-nanosheet punch-through leakage.
EOT Gate Stack
Equivalent Oxide Thickness: The theoretical thickness of pure $\text{SiO}_2$ required to achieve the same gate capacitance as a multi-layer High-κ stack ($\text{SiO}_2 + \text{HfO}_2$). Calculated as $\text{EOT} = T_{il} + T_{hk} \times (\kappa_{\text{SiO2}} / \kappa_{\text{HfO2}}) \approx 0.76\text{ nm}$.
DIBL Electrostatics
Drain-Induced Barrier Lowering: A short-channel effect where high drain voltage ($V_{DS}$) lowers the potential barrier at the source junction, shifting threshold voltage lower. Measured in $\text{mV/V}$ as $(V_{TH,lin} - V_{TH,sat}) / (V_{DS,sat} - V_{DS,lin})$.
Subthreshold Swing (SS) Electrostatics
Subthreshold Swing: The gate voltage change required to increase drain current by one order of magnitude ($10\times$) in the subthreshold region. Theoretical thermal limit at $300\text{ K}$ is $60\text{ mV/dec}$.
Inner Spacer Fabrication
Inner Dielectric Spacer: A self-aligned dielectric plug ($\text{Si}_3\text{N}_4$ or low-κ) fabricated between adjacent nanosheets under the gate edges to reduce parasitic gate-to-source/drain capacitance ($C_{gs}, C_{gd}$).
Density-Gradient Model Quantum Physics
Density-Gradient Quantum Correction: A macroscopic quantum model implemented in TCAD that solves a quantum potential correction equation derived from Wigner function moments to account for carrier quantum confinement away from oxide interfaces.
Workfunction Gate Metal Conductor
Workfunction Metal (WFM): A specific metal alloy (e.g., $\text{TiN}$) engineered to have an exact workfunction ($\Phi_m = 4.40\text{ eV}$) that sets the threshold voltage ($V_{TH}$) without requiring heavy channel doping.
